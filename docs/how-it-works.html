How a scraper gets past the Cloudflare "bouncer"

A plain-language walkthrough. Follow the arrows and the three ideas — User-Agent, fingerprint, and the cf_clearance cookie ("wristband") — all click into place.

🕷️ Step 1 — The scraper knocks
It tells the site: "Give me this page." That's a request.
↓
🛡️ Step 2 — The bouncer runs three checks
👀 Check 1 — looks must match the story
The TLS fingerprint is an "accent" you can't fake by talking — it has to look like real Chrome. The User-Agent is what you say out loud ("I'm Chrome 146"). The two must agree.
🎟️ Check 2 — do you have the "stamp"?
The hand stamp is the cf_clearance cookie. It means "already passed the bot test."
📍 Check 3 — which street are you from?
Your IP address. Arriving from "datacenter street" (e.g. GitHub Actions) is inherently more suspicious.
↓
🤔 Step 3 — Did all three checks pass?
✅
Yes → the bouncer lets you in and the scraper gets the page. Done.
❌
Stuck (e.g. no stamp) → the bouncer returns a 403 and blocks you. Time to go earn a stamp ↓
↓
🌐 Step 4 — Send a real browser to earn the stamp
Quietly launch an actual browser (Playwright), let it complete the door test like a real person, and collect the stamp (cf_clearance).
↓
🗄️ Step 5 — Save the stamp, flash it next time
Earning a stamp is expensive, so cache it and reuse it. And keep a separate stamp per site — one club's stamp isn't valid at another.
🔁 Re-knock wearing the stamp → back to Step 3 → ✅ let in
📍 One exception: when you're stuck on Check 3
If the bouncer simply dislikes that you came from "datacenter street" (your IP is blocked), no disguise or stamp helps — you need to arrive from a residential street instead (route through a residential proxy). clearway supports that too.
